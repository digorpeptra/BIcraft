src/data.rs is generated by tools/locale-oracle/gen_data.mjs (do not edit it by hand) from:

1. Unicode CLDR 48.0, as shipped in the ICU 78.3 data of Node.js 24.19.0 and read through the
   ECMAScript Intl API (month and day names where nothing was observed, the decimal and group
   separators, minimum grouping digits, percent pattern and short compact (display unit)
   forms Desktop's visuals use, and the first day of the week). Only this subset of CLDR is
   kept, as compact Rust data. CLDR is under the Unicode License v3 (LICENSE-UNICODE).

2. Results Power BI Desktop 2.158.1304.0 returned for our own probes (black-box observations):
   - tests/oracle/expected.json: DAX FORMAT, VALUE, DATEVALUE and text sort order in 59 model
     cultures (recorded against Desktop's Analysis Services engine with the maintainers'
     development oracles; corpus tests/oracle/cases.json from tools/locale-oracle/gen_cases.mjs);
   - tests/oracle/m_expected.json: Power Query conversions (Number.ToText, Date.ToText,
     Text.From, Number.FromText, Date.FromText, Currency.From) in the same cultures
     (recorded in Desktop with the maintainers' development oracles; corpus
     tests/oracle/m_cases.json from tools/locale-oracle/gen_m_cases.mjs).
   They give the separators, currency layouts, date/time patterns, AM/PM designators and the
   names DAX FORMAT and Power Query print, which take precedence over CLDR where they differ.

3. Display-unit suffixes of en, fr, de, es, it, nl and pt (K/M/bn, k/M/Md/Bn, ...): BIcraft's
   own choices, as its viewer and renderer already used them; not compared with Desktop.

Regenerate: node tools/locale-oracle/gen_data.mjs (prints the differences from CLDR).
