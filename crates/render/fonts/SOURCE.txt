The renderer's bundled fallback faces (after Selawik, in this order): glyph subsets of
Noto fonts, made by `cargo xtask font-subset bundle SRC_DIR` (xtask/src/font_subset.rs:
the release, file and SHA-256 of each source; the Unicode ranges and emoji asked for). Each face keeps
the characters of those blocks it has that Selawik and the faces before it lack, with its
glyphs renumbered; cmap of these characters only; post format 3; the GPOS kerning between
the kept glyphs written as a kern table, other layout and hinting tables dropped; name
table kept. Licence: SIL Open Font License 1.1, no Reserved Font Name;

are the upstream OFL.txt files, unmodified.
