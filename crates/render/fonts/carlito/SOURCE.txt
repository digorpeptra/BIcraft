Carlito, the renderer's stand-in for Calibri (metric-compatible: the same advance widths),
used when a visual asks for Calibri and no Calibri face is installed. Glyph subsets made by
`cargo xtask font-subset carlito SRC_DIR` (xtask/src/font_subset.rs: the files, SHA-256 and
the Unicode ranges kept): glyphs renumbered; cmap of the kept characters only; post format 3;
layout (kerning) and hinting tables dropped. Licence: SIL Open Font License 1.1, with
Reserved Font Name "Carlito": the subsets are a Modified Version, so their name table
calls them "BIcraft Metric Sans" (copyright, version, designer, URL and licence
strings kept). OFL-Carlito.txt here is the upstream OFL.txt, unmodified.
