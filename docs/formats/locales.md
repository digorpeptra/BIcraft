# Locales: formatting, parsing and collation per culture (M10.1, M10.3)

Power BI follows the model culture (`Model.Culture`, e.g. `fr-FR`) in three places BIcraft
reproduces:

- DAX `FORMAT(value, format[, culture])` and `VALUE(text)` in the engine;
- Power Query (M) conversions: `Number.ToText`, `Date.ToText`, `Text.From`, `Number.FromText`,
  `Date.FromText`, `Currency.From`, type changes, and CSV type detection;
- the order and identity of text values (VertiPaq `H$` order, relationship keys, `ORDER BY`).

Desktop's visuals format numbers their own way (CLDR separators, Power BI's display units); the
data for them is in the same crate.

Recorded against Power BI Desktop **2.158.1304.0** (Store edition), 2026-10-14. All the
observations are black-box results of our own probes.

## Crate `bicraft-locale` (layer L0)

| Item | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `Locale::get(name)` / `find(name)` | The culture's data: exact name, else the first culture of the same language (`fr-LU` → `fr-LU`, `fr-MC` → `fr-FR`), else `en-US` (`find`: `None`). `""`, `iv`, `invariant`: the invariant culture. 59 cultures. |
| `vb::format(value, fmt, locale)` | DAX `FORMAT`: named formats and custom number/date strings. |
| `parse::value(text, locale)` | DAX `VALUE`. `parse::strip_symbol` removes a currency symbol for other parsers. |
| `datefmt::format_pattern` / `format` | .NET date/time patterns (Power Query `Date.ToText`, standard formats `d D t T f F g G M Y s u`). |
| `display::{number, percent, with_unit, Unit}` | Numbers as Desktop's visuals show them (CLDR separators, display units K/M/bn per language). For the viewer and the PNG renderer. |

`Locale` fields: `decimal`, `group`, `currency` (DAX `Currency`), `pq` (Power Query's currency
layout and Indian grouping), `date_separator`, `time_separator`, the .NET-style patterns
`short_date`, `long_date`, `long_time`, `short_time`, `month_day`, `year_month`, `am`/`pm`,
`percent` (`P`), the Power Query names (`months`, `months_genitive`, `months_abbr`,
`months_abbr_genitive`, `days`, `days_abbr`), `first_day`, `vb` (what `FORMAT` prints: its
names, `Medium Date`/`Medium Time` patterns, `w`/`ww` week rules, Yes/No words) and `display`
(visuals).

### Where the data comes from

`src/data.rs` is generated by `tools/locale-oracle/gen_data.mjs` (`SOURCE.txt`):

1. **Unicode CLDR 48** (ICU 78.3 in Node 24, through `Intl`): visual separators, minimum
   grouping digits, percent layout, compact display units, first day of the week; names where
   nothing was observed. Unicode License v3 (`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`).
2. **The engine's results** for our `FORMAT` corpus (`crates/locale/tests/oracle/expected.json`,
   recorded against the Analysis Services engine of Desktop): number separators,
   currency layout, date/time patterns (derived from formatted samples and checked against
   every recorded month and weekday), AM/PM designators, the names `FORMAT` prints, week rules,
   Yes/No words.
3. **Power Query's results** for our M corpus (`crates/locale/tests/oracle/m_expected.json`,
   recorded in Power BI Desktop): the names .NET prints, `M`/`Y` patterns, `P`
   layout, `C` layout and Indian grouping.

Where Desktop and CLDR differ, Desktop wins for DAX and M. The generator prints every
difference; the main ones:

| Culture | Desktop (DAX `FORMAT`, M) | CLDR 48 |
|---|---|---|
| fr-FR group | U+202F | U+202F (same) |
| fr-BE group | `.` | U+202F |
| de-CH, it-CH group | `’` (U+2019) | `'` |
| es-ES 4-digit numbers | grouped (`1.234,50`) | not grouped (min. grouping 2) |
| de-DE abbreviated months | `Mrz`, `Jun`, `Sep` | `März`, `Juni`, `Sept.` |
| fr-FR abbreviated months | `FORMAT`: `janv`; M: `janv.` | `janv.` |
| ru-RU months | M: `Март` (nominative), `марта` (genitive) | `март`, `марта` |
| ja-JP / ko-KR M `MMM` | `3` | `3月` / `3월` |
| ja, zh, ko `FORMAT` custom tokens (`mmm`, `dddd`) | English names | native names |

## DAX `FORMAT`

Rules observed (all cultures unless noted):

- `FORMAT(x, f)` in a model of culture C gives the same text as `FORMAT(x, f, "C")` in all
  734 probes × 58 cultures; sr-Latn-RS differs on 101 probes (the model culture and the
  culture argument resolve to different cultures there; BIcraft follows the model culture).
- In custom formats `.` and `,` are the culture's separators, `/` and `:` its date and time
  separators (`dd/mm/yyyy` is `05.03.2024` in de-DE, `hh:nn` is `14.07` in fi-FI); `\/` and
  `\:` are literal.
- `Percent` and custom `%` never add a space (`25,60%` in fr-FR); `Fixed`, `Percent` are not
  grouped; `Standard`, `Currency` are.
- `Currency`: the culture's layout (`1 234,50 €`, `€ -1.234,50` in nl-NL, `($1,234.50)` in
  en-US, `-¥1,235` with 0 decimals in ja-JP).
- `General Number` / empty format: up to 15 significant digits, scientific from 1E+15 and below
  1E-04 (`1E-05`), the culture's decimal separator.
- A section whose rounded value is zero takes the zero section, or the positive one without a
  sign (`$#,0.00;($#,0.00)` of -0.004 is `$0.00`); `#,0,` scales, commas after the decimal point
  do nothing; a format with `.` keeps the separator without digits (`#.##` of 3 is `3.`);
  literals between digit placeholders sit before that many last digits (`# ##0,00`).
- Dates: `General Date`/`c` = short date + long time (time alone: long time); `ddddd` short
  date, `dddddd` long date (nominative months), `ttttt` long time; `Medium Date` and
  `Medium Time` per culture; `Short Time` is `HH:mm`.
- `mmmm`/`mmm` after a day number are genitive (`d mmmm` is `7 марта` in ru-RU).
- `AM/PM`, `A/P` print English letters; `AMPM` prints the culture's designators (empty in
  fr-FR); `am/pm` is lowercase only for a PM time with a date part (`pm` for 2024-03-05 14:07,
  `AM` for 09:05 and for a time alone).
- `w` counts from the culture's first day; `ww`: week 1 holds 1 January (en-US, ja-JP), or is
  the first four-day week with days of late December in week 1 of the next year (ISO-like,
  Europe).
- `Yes/No`, `True/False`, `On/Off` are translated (`Oui`, `Vrai`, `Actif`).

**Agreement** (`crates/locale/tests/oracle.rs`, `format_matches_the_engine`): **41 351 of 41 359
results (99.98 %)** over 701 probes × 59 cultures; every culture ≥ 99.7 %. Not counted: an
int64 beyond 2^53 (the evaluator holds numbers as doubles). Left: `AM/PM` in a few English
cultures (en-NZ, en-ZA, en-IE, en-SG print `am` for midnight).

### DAX `VALUE`

The culture's decimal separator; its group separator anywhere after the first digit (a
space-like separator also takes a plain space; en-US `1.234,5` is 1.2345); its currency symbol
before or after; sign before or after; parentheses; exponent; no `%`.

**Agreement** (`value_matches_the_engine`): **1 482 of 1 534 (96.6 %)**. Not reproduced: the
engine falls back to date/time parsing (`1.5` is 01:05 in fr-FR, `1.5` is 1 May of the current
year in ru-RU) and accepts the currency separators of de-AT (`.`) and fr-CH (`.` as decimal).

## Power Query (M)

Conversions with a culture argument use it; without one, the model culture
(`SourceOptions::culture`). `bicraft-data`'s `CultureInfo` is built from `bicraft-locale`.

- `Number.ToText` `N`/`F`/`P`/`C`/`E`/`G`/custom: the culture's separators; `P` layout per
  culture (`25,6 %` in fr-FR and de-DE, `25,6%` in nl-NL and ru-RU); `C` from Power Query's own
  currency data (de-AT `€ 1.234,50` while `FORMAT` writes `€ 1 234,50`); Indian grouping in
  en-IN and hi-IN (`12,34,567.89`).
- `Text.From` of dates and times: short date, long time, short time of the culture.
- `Date.ToText` standard formats `d D t T f F g G M Y s u o` and custom patterns, with
  genitive month names after a day number.
- `Number.FromText`: the culture's separators, plus Power Query's currency separators; a plain
  space when the group separator is U+00A0 (not for fr-FR's U+202F: `1 234,5` fails in fr-FR as
  in Desktop); the culture's currency symbol before or after (`$2.675` is 2.675 in en-US,
  `2,675 €` in fr-FR; another culture's symbol fails).
- `Date.FromText`: the culture's order; year-first cultures (ja-JP, sv-SE, en-CA, hu-HU,
  fr-CA) read `05/03/2024` month first (3 May), as Desktop does; English month names everywhere.

**Agreement** (`crates/data/tests/locale_oracle.rs`): **7 019 of 7 026 (99.9 %)** over 119 probes ×
59 cultures plus the model-culture defaults. Left: sr-Latn-RS (Power Query uses another
long date and time patterns than the engine), `Date.FromText("5.3.2024")` in id-ID and
`Date.FromText("March 5, 2024")` in vi-VN (Desktop refuses them).

### CSV type detection

`Culture::from_name` (CSV reading, `table.import`) comes from `bicraft-locale`. Numbers written
with the culture's currency symbol (`$2.675` in en-US, `2,675 €` in fr-FR) make a fixed decimal
(currency) column. Desktop's own detection step (the Power Query editor's "Detect data type")
was not driven, so this rule follows `Number.FromText` and is not verified against it.

## Collation

The linguistic collation of `bicraft-vertipaq` (`Collation::for_culture`) has a `Tailoring`
per language whose alphabet orders letters on their own: es (ñ), sv/fi (å ä ö after z, ü with y,
w with v), da (æ ø å after z, `aa` = å), nb/nn, pl, cs, sk (ch after h, č ř š ž), tr (ç ğ ı ö ş
ü; `I` is dotless), ro, hu (cs dz dzs gy ly ny sz ty zs, ö ü), lt (y after i), lv, et (z after
š, õ ä ö ü after w), hr/bs/sr-Latn (č ć dž đ lj nj š ž), sl, is (á ð é í ó ú ý, þ æ ö after
z); French cultures keep backward accents. The dictionary order (`H$`) and value identity of
PBIX writes follow it.

**Agreement** (`crates/vertipaq/tests/collation_oracle.rs`): the engine's `ORDER BY` of 145
probe words in each of 59 model cultures: **58 of 59 cultures in the same order, 7 723 of 7 728
adjacent pairs (99.94 %)**. Pairs that differ only at the third level (`æ`/`ae`, `ß`/`ss`) are
ties the query does not settle and are not counted (the `H$` order of `tests/system.rs` settles
them). Left: vi-VN (no tailoring).

## Visuals: which culture

Power BI Desktop 2.158 (Store) in English on a French Windows showed one generated report with
an en-US model and with a fr-FR model identically, in fr-FR (separators, "janv. 2025", long
dates): report visuals format in Desktop's report locale (Options > Regional settings; default
the Windows regional format), not the model culture and not the UI language. BIcraft's
renderer and viewer default to the model culture on purpose (host-independent output); `format_culture` selects another.

## Visuals (display units)

`display::Unit::suffix` gives the display-unit suffixes BIcraft draws: en `K M bn T`, fr `k M Md Bn`,
de `Tsd. Mio. Mrd. Bio.`, es `mil M mil M B`, it `k Mln Mld Bln`, nl `K mln. mld. bln.`, pt
`mil mi bi tri` (BIcraft's choices, not compared with Desktop); other languages take CLDR's short compact
forms when CLDR abbreviates by thousands, else the English ones (ja, zh, ko).

## UI phrases

The text BIcraft generates for a visual in the session's UI language (auto titles, implicit-measure
names, "(Blank)", "Total", slicer texts; `crates/engine/src/ui_lang.rs`, `docs/view-snapshot.md`
"UI language") is drawn by the viewer, the renderer and the PDF export, never written into a Power BI file. The English
and French phrases were compared with what Desktop shows. The German, Spanish, Italian, Dutch,
Portuguese and Japanese phrases are BIcraft's own translations, made from the meaning of the
English, and were not compared with Desktop: they may differ from Desktop's wording.
