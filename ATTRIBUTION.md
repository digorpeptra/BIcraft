# Attribution

Every asset and every piece of third-party data BIcraft includes, embeds at build time or shows
in its documentation, with its author, source and licence. The rules are in
[AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) §1–2. Licence texts for embedded material are under
[`licenses/`](licenses/) (identical copies of the files next to the vendored material;
`cargo xtask docs` checks them), and [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) describes
each component in full. `bicraft licenses` prints the same notices from the binary.

BIcraft contains **no Microsoft icons, logos, images, themes, sample reports or templates**, and
no Power BI files authored by others. The only Microsoft material in the repository is what
Microsoft published under the MIT licence or the SIL Open Font License, vendored unmodified and
listed below.
